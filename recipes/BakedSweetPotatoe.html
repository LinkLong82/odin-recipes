Baked Sweet Potato

Description

These baked sweet potato cubes will pair perfectly with all your favorite seasonal dinners. Roasted until tender with caramelized edges, they make a naturally sweet, savory side dish. Simple to prepare and easy to customize with herbs and spices, they’re a versatile side you’ll turn to all year long.

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil, divided
  • 3 large sweet potatoes, cut into cubes
  • 2 pinches dried oregano or to taste
  • 2 pinches salt
  • 2 pinches ground black pepper
