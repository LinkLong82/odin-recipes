Cornbread

Description

This old-fashioned cornbread dressing recipe is a simple, savory holiday side made with crumbled cornbread, sautéed onion and celery, chicken stock, eggs, and sage.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cornbread
  • Onion and celery
  • Chicken stock
  • Eggs
  • Sage

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients and preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Bake the cornbread according to the package instructions.
  3. Sauté the onion and celery in a skillet until softened.
  4. Combine the cooked cornbread, sautéed vegetables, chicken stock, eggs, and sage in a large bowl.
  5. Pour the mixture into a greased baking dish and bake for 25-30 minutes, or until set.
