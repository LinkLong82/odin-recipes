Cranberry Sauce

Description

Cranberry sauce is a traditional accompaniment to turkey during Thanksgiving and other festive meals. This recipe makes a delicious, tangy sauce that's perfect for serving alongside your main dish.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 1 cup orange juice
  • 12 ounces fresh cranberries

Steps

  1. In a medium saucepan, combine the cranberries, sugar, and orange juice.
  2. Bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
  3. Reduce the heat to low and simmer for 10-15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  4. Remove from heat and let cool for a few minutes before serving.
